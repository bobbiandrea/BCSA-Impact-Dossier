About

Bobbi Andrea

A multidisciplinary creative, technologist and builder, based in Zambia.

My work has moved across several disciplines: songwriting and music, graphic design, radio and media, onchain and community work, and now software and AI. That list can read like a series of unrelated jobs. It is closer to one practice that kept picking up new tools.

The design years taught me how things should look and why that matters. Radio taught me how to explain something unfamiliar to people who have not chosen to care about it yet. Music is the part that came first and has not stopped. The community and onchain work put all of that in front of real people who needed help getting started. The software and AI work I do now is the current chapter, not a replacement for the others.

Right now I am building SWICE, a personal AI OS with a deterministic financial execution subsystem. It is in development, not publicly available, and there is no live trading.
