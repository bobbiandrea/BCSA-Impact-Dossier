Based Creators Southern Africa

A community and ecosystem chapter I helped lead between 2025 and 2026. It is completed and no longer operating.

What it was

Based Creators Southern Africa, usually shortened to BCSA, was a regional creator and builder community operating within the wider Base ecosystem. It brought creators, students and builders together around onchain tools — through education, onboarding and in-person and online gatherings — rather than around a product.

It ran for roughly a year and then ended. This page is written in the past tense because that is what it is: a completed chapter, and a substantial one.

What I did

I held a leadership role. The work involved:

  • Community and creator coordination, including the day-to-day work of keeping a distributed group moving.
  • Creator onboarding — helping people who were new to onchain tools get started.
  • Community education and builder orientation.
  • Running regional meetings and community calls.
  • Community activations and university onboarding.
  • Ecosystem coordination within the wider Base community.

What it demonstrates

Community work is often described in numbers, and the numbers are the least interesting part of it. What this chapter actually involved was operational: coordinating people who were not employees, sustaining attention across a group with no obligation to stay, explaining unfamiliar technology to people who had no reason to trust it yet, and keeping something running without a budget or a mandate.

It is also where the creative and technical sides of my work started meeting. Onboarding creators onto onchain tools sits directly between the music and design background and the software work that came after it.

What this case study withholds, and why

The original BCSA site published membership counts, group counts, engagement figures, grant nominations and a set of dated milestones. None of those figures appear here. They have not been independently verified, and a personal portfolio is the wrong place to restate an organisation's numbers on the strength of its own marketing copy. If they can be substantiated, they can be added.

The old leadership roster is also not reproduced. Two historical sources listed different office-holders, so the record is unresolved — and other people's names and handles are not republished here without their agreement, regardless.

SongChain, which appears in the original material, was not my project. It is recorded as third-party work and no claim of ownership or involvement is made here.
