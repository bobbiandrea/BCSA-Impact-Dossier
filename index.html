Practice

Four areas, one trajectory

These areas developed at different times and are at different stages of maturity. They are listed as disciplines, not as equivalent commercial offerings.

Music & design

Songwriter, composer, producer and DJ, releasing as Bobbi Andrea and as Black Circle. Also a professional background in graphic design, which is where the visual foundation comes from.

02 / Onchain & Web3

Participation & experimentation

Experience across Base, Coinbase Wallet, Zora, Farcaster and Ethereum, including onchain music work and product experiments. Participation and exploration rather than ownership of any of them.

Education & onboarding

Community and ecosystem work in Zambia, including education, onboarding and activations. A completed leadership chapter at Based Creators Southern Africa, and a current volunteer and moderator role at Base Zambia.

Systems & engineering

The current direction. Assistant architecture, reasoning, deterministic execution, testing and failure handling — the engineering behind SWICE and earlier agent experiments.
