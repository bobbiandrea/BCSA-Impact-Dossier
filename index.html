Strategic Narrative

Southern Africa Builder Thesis

BCSA operates at the intersection of culture, education, and onchain creation.

Our regional focus prioritizes university ecosystems, creator infrastructure, and grassroots developer communities across Zambia, Malawi, and South Africa. By connecting local creative talent with global onchain platforms, BCSA positions Southern Africa as a contributor to the broader Base ecosystem.

BCSA operates as the Southern Africa Regional Square within the BasedCreators sub-DAO network under Agency DAO. We focus on creator onboarding, education, chapter support, and regional coordination on Base.
Official 5 Active Squares list: BCSA Regional Square, UNZA Chapter Square, Livingstone Chapter Square, Malawi Chapter Square, and South Africa Chapter Square.
The BCSA governance council uses a 5-seat structure: Bobbi Andrea as President, Sampa as Vice President & Treasurer, Mwimba Kanjanga as Secretary, Lady Light as Education Lead, and Base Zambia as Operations, DEV & Support.
The dossier documents community activity, chapter coordination, creator programs, university onboarding, public evidence, regional support, and projects such as SongChain.
BCSA began as a grassroots regional effort and later received ecosystem support through BasedCreators Global and Agency DAO, directed toward active contributors and practical outcomes such as onboarding, creator education, documentation workflows, artist coordination, and local creator hub experiments.
Partners, builders, creators, and community leads can support through education, creator programs, chapter activation, documentation, grants, mentorship, and regional collaboration.
