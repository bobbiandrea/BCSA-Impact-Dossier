Impact Report

A data-driven overview of Based Creators Southern Africa, covering chapter operations, ecosystem funding, and community growth across the 2025—2026 cycle.

Launched as a grassroots initiative in October 2025, BCSA has since grown into a three-country network with five active chapters and 69 community members. Scaling through ecosystem support from BasedCreators Global and Agency.vc, the chapter has successfully empowered local builders, with six members nominated for Base Builder Grants.
Growth

Active chapters across Zambia, Malawi, and South Africa, including two university-linked hubs.

Regional Strategy

BCSA prioritizes high-trust hubs by establishing chapters at universities (Evelyn Hone College, University of Zambia) where young creators and builders can learn onchain tools in a structured environment.

The Livingstone Chapter's Song Chain application serves as a real-world example of what community-funded, chapter-level development can produce. It demonstrates that Southern African builders can ship onchain products when given the right support.

Funding Transparency

BCSA operates with clear funding sources. Support from BasedCreators Global and Agency.vc DAO is deployed directly into IRL activations, onboarding workshops, and builder stipends that result in measurable onchain activity.

Six BCSA members have been nominated for Base Builder Grants, supporting projects that range from community tooling to creative applications.

Next Steps

The chapter is focused on expanding university partnerships, increasing the number of builders shipping onchain products, and submitting a formal cohort for the next round of Base Builder Grants. Cross-border coordination with Malawi and South Africa chapters continues to scale through monthly sessions.
